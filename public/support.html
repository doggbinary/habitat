Common questions

How do I draw and how do I move around? The Apple Pencil draws. One finger moves the map and drags what you placed. Pinch with two fingers to zoom, tap with two fingers to undo and three to redo.

How do I make a door? Press Play, tap a house, tap Door, then tap where it should lead. Walk into the doorway to go through.

What does Pro unlock? Export: the map PNG, the tileset PNG, character sprite sheets, map data for Unity, Godot and Phaser, and the project file. Building, saving, the character studio and Play are free.

I bought Pro on another iPad. Tap Export, then Restore purchase. It is free on every device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Where is my map saved? Inside the app on your iPad, every few seconds as you work. Export the project file to keep a copy elsewhere.
