Habitat privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Habitat is a map and character editor that works entirely on your iPad. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not track you.

Your maps and characters

Everything you make is saved on your device, inside the app. It is never uploaded anywhere. If your iPad backs up to iCloud, your map is included in that backup like any other app data. Files you export go only where you send them.

Purchases

Habitat Pro is bought through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment. To confirm a purchase and let you restore it on another device, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random identifier made by the app and the App Store's record of the purchase. It does not receive your name, email or Apple ID, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts. When you import a picture, only the file you choose is read.

Children

Habitat collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
